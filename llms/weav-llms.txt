# Weav.ai

> Weav.ai is an AI-native decisioning platform for property & casualty (P&C) insurance, applying agentic AI and document intelligence to underwriting, premium audit, and claims. It combines pre-configured knowledge graphs and scorecards by line-of-business with an agentic decision engine and human-in-the-loop review, exposed through an API-first, no-code platform. Backed by Sierra Ventures; member of the Guidewire Insurtech Vanguards program.

## Platform

- [Weav.ai Decisioning Platform](https://weav.ai/platform): AI-native decisioning for P&C insurance (Underwriting, Premium Audit, Claims).
- [Underwriting](https://weav.ai/): Risk assessment aligned to appetite, guidelines, and best practices.
- [Premium Audit](https://weav.ai/): Classification accuracy and regulatory compliance for premium audit.

## API

- Auth: `Authorization: Bearer <auth_token>`, `Accept: application/json`.
- Base URL: per-tenant environment host (the SDK prepends `env` to each service path); no single shared public production endpoint.
- Services: documents, folders, forms, agents, workflows, actions, chat.
- No public OpenAPI specification is published.
